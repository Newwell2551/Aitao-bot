Features

Everything your server needs

One bot, six core tools — click one to see it in action.

01
Welcome & Goodbye Cards
Fully custom card designs that greet new members and say goodbye — pick a layout, drop in your own background and font, Milo does the rest.
Try it — type a name
02
Custom Server Fonts
Upload your own font files (.ttf/.otf) and use them across every card — welcome, goodbye, and role cards all keep your server's own look.
03
Auto Role Assignment
Set up self-serve roles your way — Reaction is free (up to 2 roles per panel), Menu and Button unlock on Premium with more roles and richer layouts.
04
Visual Message Builder
Design embeds and announcements by clicking, not coding. Free gives you 1 simple builder; Premium unlocks unlimited builders, multiple blocks, buttons, and @mentions.
05
Animated GIF Cards
Premium
Free backgrounds are sharp PNG/JPG/WebP images. Premium adds animated GIF backgrounds — same layout, now in motion. Tap Free/Premium to compare.
06
Multi-language Support
Set with /language (admins only) — Milo replies in Thai or English per server, switching instantly, no re-setup needed. Tap Thai/English to see it run.
